The game needs a 64-bit (arm64) Android phone. The Converter is an optional companion app that turns your own MP3, WAV or FLAC files into playable songs.

Install

  1. Download the APK on your phoneTap Download APK above. When it finishes, open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  2. Allow All files accessOpen YARG and allow All files access when Android asks. YARG needs it to read your songs folder.
  3. Add songsPut song folders (Clone Hero, YARG or Rock Band format) in Internal storage/YARG/songs. Songs made with the Converter go there automatically.
  4. ScanIn YARG: Settings → Songs → Scan Songs, or in the music library: More Options → Scan Songs.
  5. Add a controller to a profilePick Gamepad: PS5 / PS4 / Xbox / other, CRKD Guitar or Other guitar. For touch buttons: Profiles → Add Device → Virtual.
Updating: if a new build won't install over the old one, uninstall the old app first. This happens when builds aren't signed with the same key. Your songs folder stays on the phone either way.
Only play songs you own (for example, charts from your own Rock Band DLC) or free and official setlists.

What's in it

Built for 64-bit phones

A 64-bit build with arm64 audio and database libraries, so it runs on 64-bit-only chips like the Snapdragon 8 Elite.

Friends' phones as guitars and mics

Other phones on the same Wi-Fi join from their browser, with nothing to install. No Wi-Fi? The host can start its own network.

Gamepads and guitars

PS5 / PS4 layout by default, plus Xbox, Switch and generic pads. CRKD and other guitar controllers too.

Guitar sound on your notes

Songs without a separate guitar track get one, played on a modelled guitar, so misses drop out just like on the real track.

Song Setup

Change speed (50–200%) and key, loop a section with a live preview, or turn on freestyle guitar.

Your own menu music

Choose a song to play on startup and a playlist for the menus, shuffled or in order.

Phone mic for vocals

Sing into the host phone's own mic, or another phone's mic over Wi-Fi.

Error logs you can send

Errors are saved to YARG/logs/yarg-errors.txt on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.

YARG Converter

A companion app that turns an MP3, WAV or FLAC into a song folder you can play right away: guitar and bass charts on Easy to Expert, Star Power, practice sections and lyrics, written into YARG/songs/Converted/.

  • Optional instrument separation makes the guitar chart from the guitar alone (a one-time 136 MB model download).
  • Lyrics can come from LRCLIB, from an .lrc file, or be pasted in and synced by tapping along.
  • Everything else runs on the phone.
